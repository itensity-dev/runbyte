Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data RunByte UAB collects through runbyte.eu, why, and what rights you have. We collect as little as we need, we do not sell data, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

01Who is responsible

The data controller is RunByte UAB, company code 308145424, registered in the Register of Legal Entities of the Republic of Lithuania. Contact for any privacy question: info@runbyte.eu.

03Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not load fonts, scripts or media from third parties.

  • Session storage. We store one value, rb2-loaded, in your browser's session storage so the intro animation is shorter when you reload the page. It contains no personal data and is deleted when you close the tab.
  • Security cookies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary cookies such as __cf_bm to tell people from bots. They are needed to keep the website secure and do not require consent.

04Who receives the data

We share data only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under a data processing agreement:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, content delivery and security of the website, including the processing of contact form submissions.
  • Email and email delivery providers: delivering contact form submissions and our correspondence with you.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example to a court or a public authority.

05Transfers outside the EU

Some providers are based in, or have access from, countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email info@runbyte.eu. We reply within one month. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, and we do not profile visitors.

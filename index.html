(04)Principles

Four rules
we don't bend.

R-01

Fixed scope, fixed price.

After discovery you get a number and a date. If the scope changes, the number changes — in writing, before the work.

R-02

You own everything.

Code, designs, cloud accounts — all in your name from the first commit. If you leave, you take it all with you.

R-03

Built to be boring.

Proven tools over shiny ones. Tests, monitoring and rollbacks before features. Excitement belongs in the product, not in production.

R-04

We stay after launch.

Software isn't finished when it's deployed. Every project comes with a support plan, and most of our clients are still with us years later.

(06)FAQ

Before you
write to us.

How much does a project cost?

Most engagements land between €25k and €250k. A paid discovery phase (from €4k) ends with a fixed price and a date. We don't do open-ended hourly work on new builds.

Do you take over existing codebases?

Yes. A large part of our work is stabilising and extending systems built by someone else. We start with a technical audit so you know what you have before we touch it.

Which time zones do you cover?

Core hours are 8:00–20:00 CET, and we've run projects with clients in New York, Dubai and Singapore without anyone waking up at 4am.

How do we communicate during the project?

A shared Slack channel, a weekly demo, and a staging link that's always up to date. You talk directly to the people building your product.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. Everything lives in your repositories and your cloud accounts.
